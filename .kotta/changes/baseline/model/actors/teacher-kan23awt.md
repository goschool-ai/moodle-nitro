# Teacher

## Role

A university teacher who runs one or more Moodle courses as editing teacher. Many are cautious and for plenty of them this is their first AI connector.

## Goals

Get the routine Moodle work of a teaching week done from the AI assistant they already use (who is behind, reminders, content, quizzes, grades), while staying in control of everything that reaches students.

## Responsibilities

Connects their AI assistant to Moodle with their own Moodle account, reads and approves every message, announcement and grade before it goes out, and revokes a connection when they no longer want it. Grades and messages are always the teacher's decision.
