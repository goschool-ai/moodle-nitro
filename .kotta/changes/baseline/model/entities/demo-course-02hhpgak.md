# Demo course

## Meaning

A teacher's personal trial course on the trial Moodle, restored from the template.

## Identity

The course, named "Demo NN" after a neutral sequence number; one per teacher.

## Attributes

- owner (editing teacher), sequence number
- the week-4 material, an assignment whose dates follow the signup day, an empty question bank
- its own fictitious students and their submissions

## Invariants

- No other teacher is enrolled in it or can see it.
- A teacher gets it once.
