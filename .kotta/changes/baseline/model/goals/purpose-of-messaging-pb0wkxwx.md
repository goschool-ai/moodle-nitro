# Purpose of messaging

## Outcome

Lets the AI reach students on the teacher's behalf through Moodle's own channels: personal messages to individual students (for example, those missing a submission) and course announcements, always after the teacher has seen and approved the text.

## Context

Individual reminders to students who are behind are part of the weekly loop.

## Baseline and target

Baseline: messages written by hand one by one. Target: one personal message per student, through Moodle's own messaging, after the teacher approved them.
