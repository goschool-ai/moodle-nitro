# Purpose of grading

## Outcome

Lets the teacher record the grades they decided on (for example, accepting a batch of submissions, or half points for late work) from the AI assistant, with every grade previewed and approved before students see it. The AI never grades on its own.

## Context

Accepting submissions is part of the weekly loop; grades are always the teacher's decision.

## Baseline and target

Baseline: grading from an AI is not possible on Moodle. Target: grades with feedback through the assign API, only after the teacher saw the preview.
