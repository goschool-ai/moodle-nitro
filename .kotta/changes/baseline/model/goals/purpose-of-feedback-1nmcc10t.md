# Purpose of feedback

## Outcome

Lets the teacher (through the AI) tell the nitro team what is missing or broken, from inside the work itself, without leaving Moodle and without the AI sending anything the teacher has not seen.

## Context

Without a pilot user, the gaps teachers hit on the sandbox are the main signal for what to build next.

## Baseline and target

Baseline: a teacher who hits a missing tool has no channel. Target: a report the teacher approved reaches the team with site and version, and nothing else.
