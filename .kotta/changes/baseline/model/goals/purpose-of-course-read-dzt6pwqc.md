# Purpose of course-read

## Outcome

Gives the AI the read access a teacher needs to answer the daily question "where does my course stand?": which courses they teach, what is in them, who takes them and whether they ever showed up, and who has submitted what.

## Context

A daily "who is behind?" check is the most frequent thing the teacher asks.

## Baseline and target

Baseline: reading needs several core functions and identity fields come along by default. Target: the course, participants with last access and submissions with grading state in a few calls, minimal by default.
