# Purpose of sandbox-onboarding

## Outcome

Lets a teacher who saw the demo try nitro on the sandbox on their own the next day: signing up gives them a personal, realistic course with fictitious students, isolated from every other attendee. Sandbox only, never part of what faculties install.

## Context

Demo attendees must be able to try Nitro the next day, on their own, without real student data.

## Baseline and target

Baseline: no place to try it. Target: sign up, confirm the email, and within a minute a personal course with 20 fictitious students.
