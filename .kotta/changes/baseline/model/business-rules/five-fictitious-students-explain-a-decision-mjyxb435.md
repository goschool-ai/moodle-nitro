# Five fictitious students explain a decision

## Rule

In every demo course, five fictitious students SHALL submit on time or late with a repository link and a short text explaining a decision they made, without a grade yet. The other students who submit with a link SHALL say no more than that the work is done.

## Rationale

The demo shows that the process, such as what decision a student made and why, tells the teacher more than the finished product (Oktatói Klub concept). The contrast with the "done" submissions gives the teacher something to find when they ask the AI which submissions show their own thinking.

## Scope

The seeded submissions of the demo assignment in each demo course. These five are kept apart from the missing, no-link and pre-graded students, and one of them is also late. It does not cover the texts' exact wording.
