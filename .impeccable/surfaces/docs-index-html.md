# nitro GitHub Pages site

Scope: the public project page at goschool-ai.github.io/moodle-nitro (`docs/index.html` Hungarian, `docs/en/index.html` English; the root sends non-Hungarian browsers to English unless the visitor chose a language). Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: teachers who saw the live demo, many of them cautious about AI, decide to try nitro on their own trial course from 2026-10-07; Moodle admins find requirements, install steps, specs and licence. No university is named anywhere on the page.

Action: before 2026-10-07 "Próbáld ki október 7-től" leads to the three steps; from 2026-10-07 the same button opens sandbox signup. Wording: "próbakurzus", never "homokozó".

Proof: an illustrated conversation (labelled "illusztráció"), no screenshots or video yet. Claims stay within PRODUCT.md: status is planning, Copilot is standards-based and not yet tested end to end.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One teaching week in one conversation, in minutes, and still personal to every student. It refuses the category default of a feature grid under a generic AI hero.

OWN-WORLD: goschool-web: warm cream ground, anthracite ink, one electric purple for action, Sora set heavy and tight for display and light for reading, Pogonia on tilted yellow and mint stickers, flat white surfaces with hairline borders, lavender for the assistant's voice.

STORY: The visitor sees the date and the week happening in a chat, believes it is fast and still their own voice with their approval, and either notes the date or copies the connector URL; an admin scrolls to install.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, "okt. 7." at 200px with the yellow "nyílik a próbakurzus" sticker, the two-line headline, the purple button. Right, a chat window with four timestamped exchanges and a preview card with "Mehet", a mint "4 perc" sticker on its corner. Nav on top.

FORM: structure 6 of the ranked list, dealt as round 2's second card, seed cce6837f.

Signature interaction: the four exchanges arrive in order with their timestamps as the page loads, and the preview card's "Mehet" is the moment the week turns personal; reduced motion shows the finished chat.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
