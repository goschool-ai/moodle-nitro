How is this different from Moodle’s built-in AI?

Moodle’s AI helps you write content in Moodle. nitro does the whole Moodle job with you, in a conversation. The two sit side by side.

  • Moodle AIGenerates text and images in the editor. nitroReads the course: submissions, late work, last logins.
  • Moodle AISummarises or explains a course page. nitroWrites pages, assignments and quizzes into the course.
  • Moodle AIOne action, where you happen to be clicking. nitroSeveral steps from one request: check, draft, send.
  • Moodle AIFor students and teachers alike. nitroFor the teacher’s week of running a course.

nitro is still in planning; Moodle’s built-in AI is available today, with the provider your admin picks. Help inside the editor stays Moodle AI’s job.

You stay in control, all the way.

nitro is not a new actor in your course: it is a tool in your hands. You work faster, in your own name, by your own rules.

With your permissions
It can do exactly what you can do in Moodle: no more, no less. Where you cannot edit, neither can it.
Nothing without your approval
Messages, announcements, grades: it shows each one first. Moodle sends it only when you say so.
Revoke it any time
Your profile shows which AI tools have access, and one click disconnects them. No copyable key that lasts forever.
Nothing in between
Data goes straight between Moodle and the AI tool you chose: none of it reaches us. Whatever you send that tool, its provider (Anthropic for Claude) processes. Every step is recorded in Moodle’s own log.

What it does in a teaching week.

It works through Moodle’s own APIs, so it can do what Moodle’s web services cannot: write pages, assignments and quizzes.

Checks

  • Who is behindsubmissions, late work, extensions
  • Who has shown up at alllast access per student
  • Where the course standssections, deadlines, hidden items

Writes

  • Pages and assignmentsfrom your own markdown, updatable
  • Questions into the bankin GIFT or Moodle XML
  • Practice quizzeswith random questions and a deadline

Sends with approval

  • Personal messagesone per student, in your voice
  • Announcementsto the course news forum
  • Grades and feedbackthat you decided on

For admins

One Moodle plugin, no intermediary service. Moodle itself becomes the MCP server, with its own sign-in.

Installation (planned)

  1. Copy the plugin to local/nitro and run the Moodle upgrade.
  2. Grant local/nitro:use to the teachers or courses that should have access.
  3. Optionally narrow the allowed AI tools in the settings. Claude and Microsoft 365 by default.
  4. The teacher pastes …/local/nitro/mcp.php and signs in with their Moodle account.

Requirements

  • Moodle 5.0 or later (4.5 comes with the pilot).
  • HTTPS. Copilot most likely also needs a one-line web server rule; the settings page checks and prints it.
  • Hungarian and English on every screen.

Status: planning. Requirements and decisions are public; the code is being built for the demo.
